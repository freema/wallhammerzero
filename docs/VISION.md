# Vision: what we want to build

A description of the project goal. This file changes only when the goal changes. Details and their dates are in [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md).

## In one sentence
A custom native **general-purpose 3D engine for large worlds**, inspired by the architecture of RAGE and GTA. All graphics in it are generated by code that AI writes.

## What we want
- **A general-purpose engine, not an engine for one game** (D15, D23). It must handle a dense GTA-style city, a large Light No Fire-style procedural planet, a Cities: Skylines-style city builder and a Stellaris-style grand strategy game.
- **A full 3D open world** with a free perspective camera: third person, a camera behind a car, free rotation (D3, D12).
- **A visual bar of at least GTA III / World of Warcraft:** native resolution, textured stylized surfaces, a dense city with buildings, cars and pedestrians (D13). No photorealism (D9).
- **Procedural generation as the core of the engine.** The world, cities, roads, buildings, vegetation, props, cars and characters come from rules. A world cell can come from pre-baked data or directly from a generator (D8).
- **No Blender and no hand modeling.** Geometry, animation and the world are code. Textures are code, or an image generated once by an AI model and then processed by code (D8, D14).
- **Procedural animation:** IK, springs and physical ragdoll (inspired by Euphoria and Overgrowth).
- **Three platforms:** development on macOS (M4 Pro), running on Linux and Windows too (D2).
- **Work through AI:** a built-in studio, MCP tools, headless mode and logs, so the engine can be controlled and debugged by machine (D17, D18).
- **One scale:** dimensions are governed by a central table and lints (D16).
- **A clean system:** everything is self-contained in the project folder (D6).

## How we work together
- The **owner** is the art director: writes specifications, supplies references and approves results.
- **Claude** is the programmer and technical artist: writes generators, lints, tests and review renders.
- **Analysis first, then code** (D11).

## What inspires us (not what we copy)
RAGE and GTA III–V (streaming, archetypes and instances, LOD), Horizon Zero Dawn (streaming, object placement), Our Machinery and Bitsquid (modularity in C), Naughty Dog (job system), Substance Designer and Houdini (procedural content), Tiny Glade and Townscaper (procedural geometry), Overgrowth and Euphoria (procedural animation).
