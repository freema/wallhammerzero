# Decisions

Only decisions **the owner made** are listed here, with the date and the owner's words
(Czech, as said). Agent proposals are not decisions: they live in research reports or in
[`tasks/2026-10-02-owner-calls.md`](../tasks/2026-10-02-owner-calls.md) until the owner
answers. A call the owner answers becomes a new row here.
