# ART SPIKE 2D: THE SAME SCENE AT 16×16 AND 16×24 (2026-10-02)

**GATE:** none · **Priority:** P1 · **Size:** L · **Depends on:** studio-verdicts, studio-animation-player

## Origin
D19: the sprite scale and view are picked after an art spike of 3 variants shown in the studio.
This note makes variants **A (16×16, 3/4 top-down)** and **B (16×24 chibi, 3/4 top-down)**;
variant C (voxel-baked iso) is `art-spike-voxel`. The spike is also the first real run of the
render → inspect → revise loop, so it measures how well it works.

## Read first
- `research/code-graphics.md` all of §1 (what Opus 5.5 does well and badly, with cases),
  §2 (best practices: style as code, rigs, low-fps animation, FX, tiles, consistency, variants,
  readability over the literal brief), §7.3 (inspection loop), §7.5 (pilot set).
- `docs/looking-at-images.md` (zoom calibration from asset-render-cli).
- The owner's `code-pixel-game` skill (`~/.claude/skills/code-pixel-game/`): the craft and the
  loop, learned on Gloop's Escape. Our CLAUDE.md and `asset-kit` win where they differ.
- `research/ref-procedural-pixel-creatures.md` §8.3 (faces drawn last as stamps at anchor points;
  an eye facing the camera is ≥ 2 px with a shine pixel; why its humanoids fail at small sizes)
  and §6.3 (what reads and what doesn't, with contact sheets in
  `research/scans/2026-10-02-procedural-pixel-creatures/samples/`).
- Kairosoft look: chibi staff and visitors, warm saturated palette, readable at 1×, 2–4-frame
  animations, pop-up FX. Do not copy Kairosoft sprites; match the feel.

## Scope
1. **Palette** `assets/palettes/spike32.json`: our own ~32 colours with named hue-shifted ramps
   (skin, hair, wood, cloth ×3, metal, plant, floor, wall, UI, FX), **hand-built** (cool
   shadows, warm highlights; ink is dark, never pure black). Shared by both variants and by
   the voxel spike.
2. **`assets/STYLE.md` v0:** logical resolution, light from top-left, outline rule, ramp rules,
   no anti-aliasing/gradients, animation timing defaults, do/don't list. Short; it grows from
   verdicts.
3. For **each** of variants A and B, in packs `spike-a/` and `spike-b/`:
   - **staff chibi:** idle (2 f), walk (4 f) in down / up / side facings (the other side is
     mirrored), work (2–3 f); params for hair, outfit and skin ramps; 3 variant seeds. Built
     from **part layers with one outline pass** (shared leg cycles, torso, head, prop); the
     **face is a stamp drawn last** at an anchor point, with an eye of ≥ 2 px and a shine
     pixel where the size allows;
   - **visitor:** idle + walk, 3 variant seeds;
   - **6 props** (desk, PC, plant, shelf, vending machine, sofa: the same list as the voxel
     spike, for a direct comparison);
   - **floor + wall tile family** with edges and `hash(x, y)` variation;
   - **1 FX:** a level-up / combo pop, 6–8 frames;
   - **scene mockup** (`kind: scene`): one ~10×8-tile room composed from the above, at 1× game
     size, so the owner judges the look in context. It includes a small **UI strip** (a panel
     and a money counter from a digit stamp set), because the Kairosoft look depends on its
     UI, and it shows the contact shadows both ways: opaque `shadow` slot and checker dither.
4. **The loop, followed and logged:** each asset gets `NOTES.md` (the "reads" at 1×, deliberate
   deviations, open issues, number of passes, rough time). Lints pass before looking; pass budget
   3 for static and 5 for animated assets; then variants, then stop.
5. `assets/spike-a/README.md` + `assets/spike-b/README.md` summarise what worked and what didn't
   (approve rate comes later from the owner's verdicts): passes and time per asset kind, which
   lints caught real defects, which zoom was needed, and what the kit is missing.

## Out of scope
- Picking the winner (owner call OC-A2-pick, after both spikes).
- Changing the owner's `code-pixel-game` skill. The spike's lessons are offered to the owner as
  a proposed diff for its lessons log and Wallhammer profile, which the owner applies (D27).
- A full bitmap font and UI kit (`runtime-text-ui`), FX as data (`fx-emitters`).
- Game logic of any kind.

## Acceptance
- Every asset in `spike-a/` and `spike-b/` bakes, passes `lint`, and shows in the studio with its
  variants; both scene mockups render at 1×.
- Each asset has a `NOTES.md` with reads, passes and time.
- Golden output hashes exist for every asset (determinism).
- A short message to the owner (via the final report, not a verdict) says where to compare A and
  B in the studio.
