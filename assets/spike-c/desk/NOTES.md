# spike-c/desk

- **Reads at 1x:** A wooden block with a lighter top on four legs; a sheet or a mug on top from the seed. The legs are one voxel (4 px) thick, so the desk reads as a solid block more than as a table.
- **Deviations from the brief:** None.
- **Open issues:** The drawer under the back edge is hidden by the top in every facing: a detail the 2D desk shows and the voxel one cannot at one voxel of depth.
- **Passes:** 1 (the contact shadow needed a margin of 5, as the example crate).
- **Rough time:** ~10 min, with the shared frame/pivot/margin table.
