# spike-c/staff

- **Reads at 1x:** Hair, skin and shirt as colour blocks, legs under them; the walk moves the legs and swings the arms; the two front facings show two 1 x 2 px eyes.
- **Deviations from the brief:** One archetype (staff), hair from brown/blond/red only: black hair is ink, and the baker shades along ramps.
- **Open issues:** At 16 x 24 the chibi is a mushroom: the head (4 x 3 x 4 voxels) is two thirds hair, the face is a sliver at 45 degrees and the eyes are stamped specks. It reads as a person, not as a face. Voxels do not help at this size (criterion c); smooth primitives would not fix the face either, the problem is the 4 px voxel against a 16 px head.
- **Passes:** 2 (the darker skin tone's ramp was missing from the slot list).
- **Rough time:** ~20 min, on the example chibi's rig.
