# new-hope/kylo

- **Reads at 4x:** A red long coat from the collar to the shins, flared to the width of the
  shoulders, a bald blue-grey head with a darker brow band on its front, dark boots. The coat's
  tail rises behind the head as a high collar, so the back facings (`ne`, `nw`) are one red slab
  and the front ones show the face. The walk shows only the boots stepping out under the hem;
  the run adds the pumped arm and the dip of the upper body.
- **Reads at 1x:** In a crowd sheet (colonists, guards, scavengers side by side, on the night
  floor and on grey) Kylo is the one red figure, the one with a pale head, and the only one
  whose outline does not narrow to two legs: told apart by colour and by silhouette in all
  four facings.
- **Deviations from the brief:** "Tox-like" is not described in the canon beyond "humanoid";
  the session chose bald, blue-grey (`steel`) skin and the high collar. The owner corrects it.
  No face at this size (D29, spike-c); the brow band is the only facial detail.
- **Open issues:** The red (`red` ramp) is his alone: colonists never wear it (their
  `gen.ts` excludes it). The `sash` param (default on) breaks the coat at the waist; `plain`
  shows it without.
- **Passes:** 4 (the rig's proportions twice, the collar and the flared hem for the silhouette,
  the brow band for the facing).
- **Rough time:** ~25 min, most of it on the shared rig in `new-hope-lib/people.ts`.
