# new-hope/kit-solid-ancient

- **Reads at 4x:** The Ancestors' smooth sand-coloured stone with no storey lines, a recessed slit per face with Psion light deep inside (`neon-violet`) or dark `psion.0`, a light lintel, a Psion vein along the left pier that jogs one voxel and runs on unbroken across storeys, a crystal on some roofs.
- **Reads at 1x:** Warm stone with violet light: unlike every other style, which is what the undercity needs.
- **Passes:** 1.
- **Open issues:** The vein at u = 0 lands on a corner column when that face is exposed, so the corner reads as a violet edge in some facings.
- **Rough time:** ~10 min.
