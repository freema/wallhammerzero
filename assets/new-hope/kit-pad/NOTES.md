# new-hope/kit-pad

- **Reads at 4x:** A dark plate with yellow dashes along its open edges and a light set into every corner where two open sides meet; the seed picks the light: green, cyan or pink. Alone (all sides open) it is a lit landing ring; a 2 x 2 pad (kit-sheet roof) has four corner lights and dashed edges.
- **Clips:** `blink`: lights on 700 ms, off 500 ms.
- **Reads at 1x:** A ring of yellow and coloured light on a roof.
- **Passes:** 3. Pass 1: a full yellow rim, a yellow target and four lamps standing on the slab: a yellow and green blob. Pass 2: dashes, no target, lamps set into the slab. Pass 3: the seed's light colour (the three variants were identical).
- **Rough time:** ~10 min.
