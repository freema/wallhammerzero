# new-hope/kit-solid-derelict

- **Reads at 4x:** Dark concrete, a window recessed one voxel per face (dark glass, a broken pane showing the dark core, rusty planks across it, or a squatter's dim lamp), a rust streak under the sill, and a broken parapet on some roofs.
- **Reads at 1x:** A dark block with a few rust marks; derelict by contrast with its neighbours.
- **Open issues:** Dark glass and a broken pane are both near-black, so "broken" reads only as "dark". The windows stay recessed on purpose (a slit reads as a hole), so they are 2 px wide.
- **Passes:** 1.
- **Rough time:** ~10 min.
