# new-hope/scavenger

- **Reads at 4x:** A hood over the head and its back, amber goggles glowing on the face row, a
  dusty or rust jacket (the other of the two from the hood), dark trousers, rust boots, and a
  big steel pack with a rust strap that rises behind the head. In the back facings the pack is
  the read: a steel block over the shoulders.
- **Reads at 1x:** A rust-and-tan figure with an amber dot and a hump behind the head.
- **Deviations from the brief:** The brief asks for a mask; the face row has room for one
  thing, so it is the goggles by default and a steel respirator with `goggles` off (the `bare`
  preset). Two variants only: the seed swaps hood and jacket between `rust` and `ancient`.
- **Open issues:** The rust and the dusty `ancient` ramps are close in value; the hood and the
  jacket read apart by hue at 4x, less at 1x.
- **Passes:** 3 (muddy rust on rust at first; then two ramps and the tall pack).
- **Rough time:** ~10 min.
