# new-hope/kit-solid-union

- **Reads at 4x:** A clean concrete block with tall two-pane glass on every face, cold office light (`light-cold`) or teal dark glass, a steel transom, a Union-blue floor line at the foot of the storey, a parapet and a vent or mast on the roof. Stacked (kit-sheet), the blue lines mark the storeys and nothing marks the cells.
- **Reads at 1x:** A pale tower block with lit windows; the floor lines and the transoms are gone.
- **Passes:** 2. Pass 1 recessed the windows one voxel: the corner pier in front hid the second column, so every window was a 2 px slit, and a Union-blue parapet cap turned the whole roof blue. Pass 2: flush windows, a concrete cap, a dark steel roof.
- **Open issues:** With `lit` 2 of 4 the bright panes dominate; `lit` is per window of four, so the mockup can lower it for a darker city.
- **Rough time:** ~20 min with the shared facade code.
