# new-hope/kit-solid-habitat

- **Reads at 4x:** Grey flats with one warm window per face (`light-warm`, sometimes `light-warm-dim`, or dark glass), and per face an AC unit, laundry on a line in cloth colours, a box of plants or nothing; a water tank on some roofs.
- **Reads at 1x:** A darker block with warm windows. The AC units, laundry and plants are 2 to 4 px each and do not read as what they are; they read as "busy", which is the point.
- **Passes:** 2 (the flush-window change of kit-solid-union).
- **Open issues:** Only one window per face: "dense flats" would need two smaller windows, which a 4-voxel face cannot hold without 1-voxel windows (2 px).
- **Rough time:** ~10 min.
