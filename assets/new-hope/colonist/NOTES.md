# new-hope/colonist

- **Reads at 4x:** A small person: hair (or a fringe) on a 2 x 2 x 2 head, a shirt with its
  sleeves, the hands, trousers of another ramp, and on about half the seeds a small bag at the
  small of the back. The face row's front is skin and the back of the head is hair, so the
  facing reads.
- **Reads at 1x:** Colour blocks: hair over shirt over trousers. The crowd is varied (green,
  yellow, violet, grey shirts; dark, tan, yellow, violet, grey hair) and never red, Union blue
  or rust, so Kylo, the guards and the scavengers stand out of it.
- **Deviations from the brief:** None. The seed picks the skin (two `skin` and two `ancient`
  tones), the hair colour and cut, the shirt, the trousers and the bag; the `bag` param forces
  it on or off (`no-bag` preset).
- **Open issues:** Shirts and trousers in the dark night ramps (`cloth`, `glass`, `hair`) start
  a step higher so they do not sink into the street; on seed 1 the figure is still the darkest
  of the crowd. No bald colonists: a bald head over a high collar is Kylo's.
- **Passes:** 4 (on the shared rig; then lighter clothes, then no bald cut).
- **Rough time:** ~15 min.
