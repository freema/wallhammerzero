# new-hope/union-guard

- **Reads at 4x:** Union blue from helmet to boots: a helmet with a lit cyan visor across the
  face row, lighter pauldrons on the shoulders, a steel belt, steel gloves and boots. The visor
  is on the front of the head, so it marks the facing; in the back facings a pixel of it shows
  at the helmet's side, which reads as the visor wrapping round.
- **Reads at 1x:** The blue figure with a cyan dot; nothing else in the pack is that blue.
- **Deviations from the brief:** None. The seed changes nothing (a uniform hides the person),
  so there is one variant seed; the `visor-off` preset shows the visor as dark glass.
- **Open issues:** The silhouette is the plain rig's (no pauldrons that stick out: the arms are
  already the outer columns of the 4-voxel width). Guards are told apart by colour, not shape.
- **Passes:** 2 (one orphan pixel where a sleeve meets the torso, fixed in the rig by
  `settleShades`).
- **Rough time:** ~10 min on the rig.
