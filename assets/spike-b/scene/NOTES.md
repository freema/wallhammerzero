# spike-b/scene

The variant B twin of `spike-a/scene` (its `NOTES.md` has the reads, the deviations and the passes of the shared drawing).

- **B-specific:** The wall is two tiles tall, so the room has six floor rows instead of seven; the props and the characters stand on the same tiles relative to the first floor row. The 16 x 24 characters fill the room better than A's; the props (16 x 24) look squat next to them.
