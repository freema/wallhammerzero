# spike-a/scene

- **Reads at 1x:** A room with a wall, a shelf, a vending machine, a plant, a desk with a PC, a sofa, two staff and two visitors, the level-up pop over the working staff member, and the UI strip with a coin, a counter in 3 x 5 digits and three stat bars. Shadows: opaque ellipses on the left half, 2 x 2 checker on the right (`shadows` param picks one).
- **Deviations from the brief:** The shadows are drawn by the scene, one row in front of the feet, for props and characters alike (the sprites' own tabs are off); the first draft hid them under the bodies. The desk moved away from the pop: the 24 px pop covers the tile behind the worker.
- **Open issues:** The counter's digits are 3 x 5 and read at 1x only as digits, not as a number one scans fast; the stat bars have no icons.
- **Passes:** 2 visual passes on `context.png`.
- **Rough time:** ~30 min, with the digits.
