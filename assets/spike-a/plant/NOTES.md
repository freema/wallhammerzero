# spike-a/plant

- **Reads at 1x:** A round green bush on a pot (wood or red from the seed); the seed shifts the side blobs.
- **Deviations from the brief:** A bush, not three leaves: three separate leaves left holes the outline turned into specks (`orphan-pixels`) and went over the top edge (`fits-frame`).
- **Open issues:** At 16 x 16 the pot and the bush are each a blob; a stem of 2 px is all that separates them.
- **Passes:** 3.
- **Rough time:** ~15 min.
