# New Hope: the story

The source is the owner's canon *For the Empire* ("Alpha Frontier: master chronicle") in the
owner's Vellum, `inbox/for-the-empire.md`, chapters "Andromeda, New Hope and Kylo found again"
and "The fall of the Black Guard … Kylo's passage". Sessions read it there and never write to
it (D27, OC-D1). This file keeps only what the game uses. **Canon** is what the chronicle says;
**proposal** is a session's idea that waits for the owner.

## Canon

- **New Hope** is a habitable world at the end of a hyperspace route that leads *out of* the
  main galaxy, to a small galaxy known by the working name **Andromeda** (not the real one).
- Its **whole surface is covered by vast cities and enormous high-rise buildings**. When it was
  found it was **completely abandoned**.
- It is an old world tied to both the **Ancestors** (Předkové) and the **Psions** (Psioni). Who
  built the cities, how old they are and why the planet was abandoned are unknown.
- The **Union** (Unie) found it first, named it New Hope and began to **resettle the empty
  cities** step by step.
- While colonising, the Unionists found a **capsule in space**. Inside was **Kylo**.
- Kylo is an **ascendant Ancestor**: he looks like a Tox but is not one. He can **pass into or
  incarnate in other bodies**. He does not remember how he got into the capsule or into this
  part of space.
- In the chronicle's later timeline Kylo kills the Tox emperor, walks through the emperor's
  artefact (a tunnel to another galaxy) and **turns up at New Hope**; nobody knows why, and
  whether it was chance or something led him there is an open question of the canon.
- Open in the canon: the real name of the small galaxy, who built the cities, why the planet
  was abandoned, its ties to the Ancestors and the Psions, who made the capsule and where it
  came from, why Kylo lost his memory.

## Proposal: how the game uses it

Waits for the owner's word; the concept in [README.md](README.md) builds on it.

- **The player is Kylo**, woken from the capsule in a Union facility high in the city, with no
  memory. The open questions of the canon are the game's mystery: the game answers some of
  them only with the owner.
- **Up is the Union, down is the past.** The top levels and the sky lanes are the colonised
  city: neon, traffic, Union security. Below them the half-settled middle levels of scavenger
  crews and gangs. At the bottom the dark streets, and under them the undercity of the
  Ancestors and the Psions, untouched since the planet emptied. Going down is going into the
  mystery.
- **Kylo's body-passing is the GTA verb.** Where GTA takes cars, Kylo can also take bodies:
  pass into a Union guard to get through a checkpoint, into a pilot to take a flying car. The
  cost and the limits are open (memory loss, time, a trace the Union can follow).
